# Booking product images

## Branded drinks and snacks

The 19 branded product images were downloaded from [Shop Suki](https://shopsuki.ph/) on 2026-11-21 and stored locally for the Drinks / Junk Foods catalog. The exact product page, original image URL and local filename for each item are recorded in [product-sources.json](product-sources.json). The downloaded versions request 320px images from the retailer's image CDN. Product photography belongs to its respective owners; no open license is asserted here. Packaging and flavors are reference images, and staff can replace them with photos of venue stock in Admin → Add-ons & Food.
